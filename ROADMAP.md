# Roadmap (proposed)

- v0.1.0: local CSV engine, expense and payroll arithmetic, budget matching, skill, tests.
- v0.2.x: payroll statutory rules after independent validation (INSS, IRRF, FGTS, férias, 13º, overtime, regimes, collective agreements); reference fixtures and versioned competence.
- v0.3.x: isolated RTC adapter; tax retention modules only after legal review.
- v0.4.x: grant and donor policies, procurement, cost allocation, indirect costs, FX with documented source, timesheets, evidence controls.
- v0.5.x: optional read-only MCP tools; typed inputs, local authentication and authorization, audit logs, least privilege, user-approved exports. No payment tools.
- v1.0: private dashboard, optional XLSX import, role-based access and security review; independently validated rule packs.

Dates are not commitments. Do not represent planned modules as available.
